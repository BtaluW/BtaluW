26 Aug 2026
week 35

Five templates shipped, and a cache that did not know they existed

Registered five named resume templates and retired the old generic one, to clear a hard requirement: at least five templates rated ATS-friendly. Building them surfaced a real bug: applying a new template did not invalidate the public share-link's cached PDF, so a candidate switching templates could keep serving their old design to anyone with the link, indefinitely.

27 Aug 2026
week 35

A PDF that was 3.5% smaller than it should have been, silently

A banner résumé template pulled its header toward the page edge with a negative margin — looked right in the browser canvas. In the exported PDF, Chromium's print engine clipped the bled content entirely and silently shrank the whole document to fit, with no error. Fixed by shrinking the page's own padding instead of fighting it with a negative margin.

13 Aug 2026
week 33

AI resume suggestions, narrowed on purpose

The suggestions/rewrite feature only round-trips on text that already exists in the resume, not free-form generation. Contact fields (email, phone, name) are excluded from what ever reaches the prompt, even if someone tried to pass one in.
